# Stylized Casual Chess Set

Twelve individual pieces in orange and blue, a board, and a complete 32-piece starting position. All 14 GLBs are self-contained and retain their original vertex colours and normals.

Use the individual files to build your own board, or start with glb/starting-position.glb. Units are metres, with glTF Y up. Individual pieces are centred at their bases and grounded at zero. The board measures 0.434 × 0.434 × 0.017 m; squares measure 0.050 m. Assembly piece bases sit on the board at 0.017 m.

The files contain no image textures. Preserve COLOR_0 vertex colours and imported normals. The starting position uses 13 shared meshes and 33 mesh nodes; 25,030 drawn triangles. No chess logic, animation, collision shapes, or engine integration is included.
